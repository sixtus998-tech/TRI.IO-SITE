About the group

Built as one group, operating across nine sectors.

DSIC grew from a simple premise: that a lean group holding company, disciplined about its numbers, can move into any sector where it can serve clients well.

Our mission

To deliver dependable, well-run services across consultancy, data, telecommunications, publishing, education, trade, technology and contracting — under a single standard of accountability.

Our vision

To be one of Nigeria's most trusted diversified institutes — known as much for the discipline of our record-keeping as for the quality of our divisions' work.

How we operate

One group, nine ledgers, one record

Every division — from Consultancy to Contractor Services — logs its own revenue and expenses, but all of it lands in a single group ledger. That's what lets leadership see the whole group's health at a glance, and what lets each division stay focused on its own work.

Integrity first

Every entry in the group ledger is attributed to the staff member who logged it — nothing moves through the books anonymously.

Sector discipline

Each division is run by people who understand that sector — consultancy is run like consultancy, contracting like contracting.

Shared accountability

Group-wide totals are visible to every staff member — not siloed behind any one division or office.
