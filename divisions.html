Nine divisions, one group

Every division tri.io operates.

Each division below runs its own work and its own client relationships. All nine report their revenue and expenses into the same group ledger.

01Consultancy+
Advisory

Strategic and operational advisory services for institutional and private clients, drawing on cross-division expertise from across the group.

02Data Analysis+
Insight

Data collection, analysis and reporting services supporting both external clients and the group's own decision-making.

03Telecommunication+
Infrastructure

Connectivity and telecommunications services, including infrastructure that supports other divisions' operations.

04Publishing+
Content

Educational and institutional publishing — from learning materials to reports produced for external partners.

05School Mobile Tutor (Private)+
Education

Private, mobile tutoring services delivered directly to students and families outside the classroom setting.

06Student Welfare+
Education

Welfare support for students in the group's care, run as its own division alongside the tutoring service.

07Import & Export+
Trade

Cross-border trade operations, moving goods and materials in support of the group and external clients.

08I.C.T+
Technology

Information and communications technology services, from systems support to technology delivery for clients.

09Contractor+
Projects

Project and contracting work carried out on behalf of institutional clients across sectors.
